# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant 2.1 standard of a welcoming, respectful community. Its full policy is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

Treat contributors with respect regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Use inclusive language, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what benefits the community.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions or restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement considers severity and context and may include correction, warning, temporary exclusion or permanent exclusion.

Report incidents privately through the maintainer's contact method on the Bojan-Ivanovski GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers are responsible for fair and consistent enforcement and may be contacted privately about concerns with a decision.
